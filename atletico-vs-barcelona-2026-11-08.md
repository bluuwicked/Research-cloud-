## 📋 TACTICAL SHAPES & IDENTITIES
**Atlético Madrid (Simeone): 4-4-2**
- *Out of possession:* A compact 4-4-2 mid-block. Giuliano Simeone (right) and Baena (left) tuck in to close the half-spaces. Koke (back in for Hjulmand) and Barrios screen Zone 14.
- *In possession:* The shape becomes a 2-4-4 / 3-2-5 on the counter. Llorente surges up from right-back, Grimaldo overlaps from left-back, and Lee Kang-In drops off Julián Álvarez to receive between the lines.
- *Identity:* Win the ball in midfield, then go vertical fast into the space behind Barcelona's high line. Álvarez is both the link player and the man running in behind.

**Barcelona (Flick): 4-3-3, single pivot**
- *Out of possession:* A high line with an aggressive counter-press. The back four is Cancelo, Martín, Cubarsí and Fariñas.
- *In possession:* A 3-2-5 / 2-3-5 shape:
  - Cancelo inverts from left-back, with Bernal at the base of midfield.
  - Pedri and Fermín play in the half-spaces.
  - Yamal holds the right touchline, Gordon stretches the left, and Raphinha plays as a false 9.
- *Identity:* Control territory, overload the right through Yamal, and press immediately after losing the ball. The weakness is a high line with no Rodri or Koundé.

## ⚔️ THE BATTLEGROUND: WHAT HAPPENED IN THE FIRST HALF
**1. Barcelona's build-up vs Atlético's mid-block**
- Barcelona had the ball (58% possession; 281 accurate passes to 187). Cubarsí made 68 accurate passes and 7 passes into the final third, so the build-up ran through him.
- Atlético pressed on specific triggers: passes back toward the Barcelona box, and the full-backs receiving under pressure.
  - On 26' they forced a Cancelo error 30 yards out, and Joan García had to save from Álvarez through his legs.
  - On 42' Álvarez nearly robbed Fariñas on a backward pass.
- Atlético won the ground battle: 27 ground duels won to 17 (61%), and 13 tackles to 6. Barrios alone won 9 duels.

**2. Final-third creation vs the block**
- **Barcelona's route** was Yamal against Grimaldo:
  - Yamal: 3 dribbles completed, 3 chances created, 7 duels won.
  - He went past Grimaldo on 17' and 23', and Baena was booked (43') for stopping him.
  - The left side gave Barcelona nothing. Gordon (5.6 rating) and Raphinha (5.8) had no chances created and no dribbles between them.
  - Atlético's block took the sting out of the possession: 4 Barcelona shots were blocked. Barcelona had 18 touches in the box but only 0.75 xG.
  - The equaliser (45') came from Pedri carrying the ball through midfield. Fermín's pass came back to him off Llorente, and he hit it first time into the top corner. It was worth only 0.21 xG.
- **Atlético's route** was runs in behind and the switch of play:
  - Álvarez: 0.62 xA from 2 chances created.
  - **Goal (10'):** Llorente broke down the right and switched play with the outside of his boot. Álvarez passed up his own shot and set up Giuliano Simeone (0.69 xG).
  - **6':** Koke played Álvarez through and the chance was nearly identical, but Martín intercepted the square pass.
  - **38':** Baena's 0.47 xG chance was saved.

**3. Transitions and rest-defence**
- This is where the half was decided. Barcelona's rest-defence is Bernal plus two centre-backs, with Cancelo inverting from left-back. That left Atlético room to break through the right channel and through the space Fariñas vacated when he pushed high.
- Atlético had 3 big chances to Barcelona's 1, and almost all of their 1.38 xG came from open play (1.36).
- Both teams hit the woodwork once: Pubill's header from a corner (6') and Pedri's shot (7').
